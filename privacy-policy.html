Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Welcome to ToolRivo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information that may be collected when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

ToolRivo provides online tools that are designed to work directly in your web browser. Many tools process files or text locally in your browser and do not require you to create an account.

We may collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type, device type, approximate usage information and pages visited, if analytics or advertising services are enabled.

2. Files and Text

When you use tools such as image compressors, image converters, calculators or text tools, files and text may be processed directly in your browser where technically possible.

You should avoid entering passwords, confidential documents, financial information or other sensitive information into any public online tool.

ToolRivo may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, analytics and advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Some ToolRivo features may use third-party services. For example, the QR Code Generator may use an external QR generation service. YouTube thumbnail images may also be loaded from YouTube's image servers.

These third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing their policies when using those services.

5. Analytics

ToolRivo may use analytics services to understand website traffic, popular tools and general visitor behavior. Analytics information is used to improve the website and its tools.

6. Advertising

ToolRivo may display advertisements from third-party advertising networks in the future. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide relevant advertisements and measure performance.

7. External Links

ToolRivo may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

8. Children's Privacy

ToolRivo is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the website.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
